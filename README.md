# STYLE WEAR — NFC Digital Brand Profile

A single-page, mobile-first digital business card that opens when someone
taps a Style Wear NFC card or scans its QR code.

Designed & developed by NovExa Tech.

---

## What this is

- **A digital brand profile**, not a website.
- Frontend-only: HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript.
- No backend, no database, no API, no CMS.
